## The legal test you must satisfy

A national interest waiver waives the job-offer and PERM labor-certification
requirements that normally attach to an EB-2 petition
(INA § 203(b)(2)(B)(i); 8 C.F.R. § 204.5(k); USCIS Policy Manual Vol. 6, Pt. F, Ch. 5).

**Step 0 — You must first qualify for EB-2 itself.** Either:
- **Advanced degree:** U.S. master's or higher, or foreign equivalent; **or** a
  U.S. bachelor's (or foreign equivalent) **plus 5 years of progressive
  post-baccalaureate experience** in the specialty; **or**
- **Exceptional ability:** meet ≥ 3 of the 6 criteria at 8 C.F.R. § 204.5(k)(3)(ii).

**Step 1 — *Matter of Dhanasar*, 26 I&N Dec. 884 (AAO 2016).** Three prongs,
all three required:

| Prong | What it asks | Where it is argued |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | The **proposed endeavor** has both **substantial merit** and **national importance** | `03_PETITION_LETTER.md` § IV + `04_PROPOSED_ENDEAVOR_STATEMENT.md` |
| **2** | You are **well positioned to advance** that endeavor | `03_PETITION_LETTER.md` § V + letters + exhibits |
| **3** | **On balance** it benefits the U.S. to waive the job offer / labor certification | `03_PETITION_LETTER.md` § VI |

**The single most common failure:** describing your *occupation* ("I am a project
engineer") instead of a **specific proposed endeavor** ("I will develop and
execute cost-and-constructability engineering for modular capital projects at
U.S. Gulf Coast petrochemical facilities, with emphasis on X"). USCIS's January
2025 policy update on NIW is explicit that the endeavor must be described
specifically. Prong 1 is judged on the **endeavor**, not on the job title, and
not on the general importance of the industry.

## One caution specific to your profile

Process engineering in the U.S. energy sector is a **well-populated occupation
with an established PERM path**, and USCIS officers know it. Prong 3 is where
petitions like yours are lost. Your case does not win on "energy is important to
America" — every officer has seen that. It wins on **specific, documented,
attributable contributions of yours**, plus a concrete reason a labor
certification is impractical for the endeavor you propose.
