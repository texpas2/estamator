# 10 — Employment Verification Letters

> These prove the experience underlying your EB-2 classification (especially the
> bachelor's + 5 years progressive route). They are a **different document** from
> a recommendation letter: no opinions, no praise — dates, titles, duties, hours,
> and the increase in responsibility.

## Requirements — 8 C.F.R. § 204.5(g)(1)

A qualifying experience letter must be from a **current or former employer** and
must state:
- the **exact dates** of employment (from / to, month and year);
- the **job title(s)** held;
- a **detailed description of the duties** performed;
- that the employment was **full time** (state hours per week);
- the writer's **name, title, and contact information**, on company letterhead.

If a former employer is defunct or refuses, you may substitute your own
**affidavit** plus affidavits from **former colleagues** who worked with you,
explaining why the employer letter is unavailable. Document the attempt to obtain
it — save the emails.

## Practical notes

- **Ask HR and the manager separately.** Many companies' HR will only confirm
  dates and title. That is not enough — you need the duties paragraph, which
  usually has to come from your former manager. It is normal to file one HR
  letter (dates/title/full-time) **plus** one manager letter (duties).
- **Progression must be visible.** If two titles have identical duty lists, the
  letter does not establish progressive experience. Have the writer articulate
  what changed.
- **Match the letters to the timeline in the brief.** Any gap or overlap between
  letters will be noticed. Reconcile them before filing.
- **Get these while people still remember you.** Managers move companies. Ask now,
  even if you file in six months.
