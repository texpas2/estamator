# 06 — Five-Year Plan for the Proposed Endeavor

> *Dhanasar* prong 2 expressly considers "a model or plan for future activities"
> and "progress towards achieving the proposed endeavor." 26 I&N Dec. at 890.
> A plan with dates, counterparties and numbers is evidence. A plan of ambitions
> is not.
