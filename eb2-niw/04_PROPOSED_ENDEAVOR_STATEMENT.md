# 04 — Statement of Proposed Endeavor

> This is the most important page in the filing. USCIS's NIW policy guidance
> requires the endeavor to be described **specifically** — not as an occupation,
> not as an industry. Prong 1 is evaluated against *this text*. Write it first;
> everything else keys off it.

---

## 1. The endeavor in one sentence

> *Fill this in. It must survive the "so what does he actually do?" test.*

```
I propose to {{VERB: develop / apply / advance}} {{SPECIFIC TECHNICAL WORK}}
for {{SPECIFIC CLASS OF FACILITY}} at {{SPECIFIC U.S. GEOGRAPHY}}, in order to
{{SPECIFIC MEASURABLE OUTCOME}}.
```

**Worked example of the right shape** *(replace entirely with your true facts —
this is an illustration of form, not a claim about you)*:

> *I propose to develop and apply front-end cost, constructability and modular
> execution engineering for new and revamped process units — {{unit types}} — at
> petrochemical and refining facilities on the U.S. Gulf Coast, in order to
> reduce installed capital cost and field construction exposure hours on domestic
> capacity additions, and to transfer these methods to U.S. engineering and
> estimating teams through {{documented mechanism: standards, training,
> published benchmarks}}.*

**Test your sentence against these four questions.** If any answer is "it doesn't
say", rewrite it.

| Question | Your sentence must answer |
|---|---|
| *What technical work, precisely?* | Not "engineering." Name the deliverables. |
| *Applied to what?* | Name the facility class and unit types. |
| *Where in the U.S.?* | Name the region and why it is that region. |
| *Producing what measurable outcome?* | A number or a class of number. |

## 6. Common failure modes — check your draft against these

| ✗ Do not write | ✓ Write instead |
|---|---|
| "I will work as a project engineer in oil and gas." | The four-part sentence in § 1. |
| "The energy industry is critical to the U.S. economy." | What *your endeavor* changes about it, with a number. |
| "I will use my skills to benefit American companies." | Which companies, which facilities, which deliverable. |
| "My work is world-class." | The measured result and who else adopted it. |
| A description that would fit 40,000 other engineers. | A description that fits your documented record. |
