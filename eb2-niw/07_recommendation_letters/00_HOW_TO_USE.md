# 07 — Recommendation Letters

## The rules that matter

1. **Independence is the whole game.** A letter from your own boss proves you did
   your job. A letter from someone with **no employment, contractual, academic or
   personal relationship to you**, who nonetheless knows your work, proves it
   matters. Target **at least 3 of 5–7 letters as independent.**
2. **Signed on official letterhead**, with the recommender's full title, contact
   details, and their **CV or bio attached behind the letter**. A letter without
   the writer's credentials proves nothing about the weight of the opinion.
3. **Vary them.** Officers read these all day and detect a single author
   instantly. Different lengths, different structures, different vocabulary,
   different emphases. Let each recommender rewrite in their own voice — send the
   draft as a *starting point*, and say so explicitly in your request.
4. **Specific > laudatory.** One sentence that says "his quantity benchmarking
   model was adopted as {{Company}}'s standard basis for Class 3 estimates on
   subsequent Gulf Coast projects" outweighs three paragraphs of "outstanding."
5. **Each letter must touch the prongs.** Not in legal language — in the
   recommender's own terms: what the work is, why it matters beyond the project,
   and why this person is positioned to keep doing it.
6. **Do not have all letters address all three prongs identically.** Assign each
   recommender the part they can actually speak to.

## Letter portfolio

| # | File | Recommender profile | Independent? | Primary prong |

| 1 | `L1_independent_industry_expert.md` | Senior technical authority at an owner-operator or EPC with no tie to you | **Yes** | 1 & 2 |
| 2 | `L2_independent_academic.md` | Professor / researcher in construction engineering, engineering economics, or process design | **Yes** | 1 |
| 3 | `L3_independent_association.md` | Officer of AACE International / AIChE / CII / PMI chapter or committee | **Yes** | 1 & 3 |
| 4 | `L4_former_supervisor.md` | Your project or engineering manager | No | 2 |
| 5 | `L5_client_owner_operator.md` | Client-side manager who received your work | Partly | 2 & 3 |
| 6 | `L6_peer_collaborator.md` | Discipline lead / peer from another company on a shared project | Partly | 2 |

## Timeline

Independent recommenders take **4–10 weeks**. Send requests before you draft
anything else. Follow up at week 2 and week 4. Have a seventh candidate in
reserve — one will always fall through.

## How to find independent recommenders when you don't already know any

- AACE International section meetings (Houston section is large and active) —
  present your work, then ask.
- CII / UT Austin research teams working on modularization and front-end planning.
- Authors of papers you have actually used in your work: write to them, send your
  method, ask for their technical assessment. Many will engage.
- Standards committee members (API, ASME) in your area.
- Former clients who are now at other companies — the employment relationship has
  ended, which strengthens independence.

**Do not buy letters, and do not use a service that supplies recommenders.**
USCIS is alert to it, and a fabricated relationship is fraud.
