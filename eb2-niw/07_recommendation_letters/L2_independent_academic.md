# Letter 2 — Independent Academic / Researcher

**Profile:** Faculty or senior researcher in construction engineering &
management, engineering economics, chemical/process engineering, or capital
project performance research (e.g. a CII-affiliated researcher). No academic,
advisory or personal relationship with you.

**Purpose:** This letter carries **prong 1**. An academic can situate your work
within the documented, published problem in a way no practitioner letter can, and
can cite literature. Ask them explicitly to cite their own or others' published
research — that turns their letter into a bridge to your independent exhibits.
