# Request Email Template

> Send as plain text. Attach: your CV, the one-sentence endeavor statement, and a
> one-page summary of the specific work you want them to comment on.
> **Explicitly invite them to rewrite.** A letter in their own words is worth more
> than a polished one in yours.

---

**Subject:** Request for a technical reference letter — {{YOUR NAME}}

Dear {{Dr./Mr./Ms.}} {{Last Name}},

{{One or two sentences: how you know their work, or how you came to contact them.
Be specific — cite their paper, their talk, the committee, the project. Generic
openings get ignored.}}

I am preparing a petition to U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services for an
employment-based immigrant classification (EB-2) with a national interest waiver.
This category asks for independent technical assessment of an applicant's work
and of its importance to the United States, and I am writing to ask whether you
would be willing to provide such a letter.

Concretely, I would be asking you to comment on:

- {{The specific technical contribution you want them to evaluate — one line.}}
- {{Why that class of work matters to U.S. {{industry/infrastructure}} — one line.}}

I have attached my CV, a one-page description of the work, and — only to save you
time — a draft that you should feel free to discard entirely or rewrite in your
own words. Your own assessment, in your own voice, is far more valuable than
anything I could draft, including any part where you disagree with my
characterization.

The letter would be on your letterhead, signed, with your CV or biography
attached. If it would help, I am glad to have a short call first.

I understand this is a real request on your time, and I will take a "no" without
any awkwardness.

With thanks and regards,

{{YOUR NAME}}
{{title}} | {{email}} | {{phone}}
{{LinkedIn / publications page}}
