# Letter 4 — Former Supervisor / Engineering Manager

**Profile:** The project manager, engineering manager or chief estimator you
reported to on {{PROJECT_EXXON_NAME}} or {{PROJECT_BOFLUX_NAME}}.

**Purpose:** prong 2 — direct, first-hand, quantified verification of what you
personally did. This letter is the factual foundation the independent letters
rest on. It is *not* the letter that wins the case, and it should not try to be:
ask them for facts and numbers, not for praise.

**Confidentiality warning:** they are describing employer project work. Have them
confirm with their company what they may disclose, and redact commercial terms.
