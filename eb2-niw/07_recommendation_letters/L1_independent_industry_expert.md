# Letter 1 — Independent Industry Expert (strongest letter in the package)

**Profile:** A senior technical authority — chief estimator, engineering
director, VP of capital projects, technical fellow — at an owner-operator, EPC
contractor or licensor, who has **never employed you, never been employed with
you, and has no personal relationship with you**, but who knows your work through
{{a publication, a conference presentation, an industry committee, a benchmarking
exchange, or review of your method at their own company}}.

> Give this recommender the substance and let them write. Their independence is
> the evidence; the prose is secondary.
