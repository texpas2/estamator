# Letter 6 — Peer Collaborator from Another Organization

**Profile:** A discipline lead, licensor engineer, fabricator's engineering
manager, or consultant from a **different company** who worked alongside you on a
shared project. Partly independent — no shared employer, but a shared project.

**Purpose:** prong 2 corroboration from outside your own company's chain of
command. Cheap to obtain, and it fills the gap between "my boss said so" and a
fully independent expert.
