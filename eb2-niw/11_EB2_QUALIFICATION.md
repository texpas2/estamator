# 11 — Establishing EB-2 Eligibility (the threshold step)

> Before *Dhanasar* is even reached, you must qualify for EB-2 classification.
> Petitions are denied at this step, on evidence the petitioner could easily have
> supplied. Resolve this first.

---

## A. Route 1 — Advanced degree

**8 C.F.R. § 204.5(k)(2):** an "advanced degree" is any U.S. academic or
professional degree **above a baccalaureate**, or a foreign equivalent degree.

**Evidence required:**
- Official academic record showing the degree *(Exhibit 1)*
- Transcript *(Exhibit 2)*
- Certified English translations of both
- Credential evaluation from a recognized evaluator *(Exhibit 3)*

**Your likely issue.** If your degree is a post-Soviet five-year **Specialist**
(спеціаліст / специалист) or a Ukrainian/Russian Master's, the evaluation outcome
is not automatic:

| Credential | Common U.S. evaluation | Practical effect |
|---|---|---|
| 4-year Bachelor (bakalavr) | U.S. bachelor's | Need Route 2 (+5 yrs) |
| 5-year Specialist (pre-Bologna) | **U.S. bachelor's** most often; sometimes bachelor's + some graduate study | Usually need Route 2 |
| Magistr (Master's, post-Bologna) | U.S. master's | Route 1 works |
| Candidate of Sciences | U.S. Ph.D. | Route 1 works |

**Action:** order the evaluation **now** from a NACES-member evaluator
(WES, Josef Silny, SpanTran, Educational Perspectives). Turnaround is typically
2–8 weeks; expedited service is available. Ask specifically for a
**course-by-course** evaluation — a document-by-document evaluation is not
sufficient for USCIS purposes here.

> If your evaluation comes back as "bachelor's equivalent," that is not a
> problem — it just means you file under Route 2. Do not shop for a more generous
> evaluator; inconsistent evaluations are noticed and damage credibility.

## B. Route 2 — Baccalaureate + five years of progressive experience

**8 C.F.R. § 204.5(k)(2):** a U.S. baccalaureate or foreign equivalent
**followed by at least five years of progressive experience** in the specialty is
considered the equivalent of a master's degree.

**Four things must be shown, and each is a distinct proof:**

| Requirement | How you prove it |
|---|---|
| Bachelor's or foreign equivalent | Credential evaluation *(Exhibit 3)* |
| Experience is **post-baccalaureate** | Dates on the degree vs. dates on employment letters must not overlap in the counted period |
| **Five years**, full time | Employment letters stating hours/week *(Exhibits 4–9)* |
| **Progressive** | The letters must show increasing responsibility, not repetition |

**"Progressive" is where petitions fail.** Build this table and make the letters
match it:

| Period | Title | Scope of authority | What increased |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{dates}} | {{Estimator I}} | {{discipline packages ≤ ${{X}}M}} | — |
| {{dates}} | {{Senior Estimator}} | {{full unit TIC, Class {{N}} basis}} | {{scope, estimate class, independence}} |
| {{dates}} | {{Lead / Project Engineer}} | {{multi-discipline, team of {{N}}, sanction-gate accountability}} | {{supervision, decision rights}} |

## C. Route 3 — Exceptional ability (plead in the alternative only if you meet ≥3)

**8 C.F.R. § 204.5(k)(3)(ii)** — you must satisfy **at least three**:

| # | Criterion | Do you meet it? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Official academic record showing a degree relating to the area of exceptional ability | ☐ | Diploma |
| B | Letters from current or former employers showing **at least 10 years** of full-time experience in the occupation | ☐ | Employment letters |
| C | A **license to practice** the profession or certification for the profession | ☐ | P.E. license; AACE CEP/CCP |
| D | Evidence that you have **commanded a salary or other remuneration** demonstrating exceptional ability | ☐ | Pay records + BLS OEWS comparison for your SOC and MSA |
| E | Membership in **professional associations** | ☐ | AACE, ASME, AIChE, PMI |
| F | **Recognition for achievements and significant contributions** by peers, government entities, or professional/business organizations | ☐ | Awards, adoption evidence |

> For a Texas-based project/cost engineer with a P.E. and 10+ years, criteria
> **B, C, E** are often already met, with **D** available if your salary exceeds
> the BLS 75th percentile for your SOC code in the Houston MSA — pull the OEWS
> data and check. **Pleading exceptional ability in the alternative costs you
> nothing and gives the officer a second path to approve you.** Do it if you
> qualify; do not stretch to it if you don't.

**10 years of progressive post-baccalaureate experience**, against a 5-year
requirement. The progression is genuine and easy to document: individual
contributor → team lead at a design institute → owner-side discipline authority
at a Shell joint venture. That is exactly the shape 8 C.F.R. § 204.5(k)(2) wants.

**The 2019–2025 gap does not defeat Route 2.** The regulation requires five years
of progressive experience following the baccalaureate; it does not require the
experience to be continuous or recent. Do not let anyone tell you otherwise. The
gap is a **prong 2** problem, not an eligibility problem — see file `12`.
