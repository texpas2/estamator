## Translation requirement

8 C.F.R. § 103.2(b)(3): any document in a foreign language must be accompanied by
a **full English translation** that the translator certifies as complete and
accurate, together with the translator's certification of competence to
translate. Attach the certification **to each document**, not once at the front.
