### Notes on tone

- Write like an engineer, not like a brochure. Numbers, units, dates, standards.
- Never write "I am one of the few people in the world who…" unless you can cite
  the count. Officers read that sentence a hundred times a week.
- Do not thank the officer or plead hardship. It is not a hardship application.
- Read it aloud. If a sentence would embarrass you in a design review, cut it.
